Inside Couple Rodeo

More than another place to message.

Each feature has a small, clear purpose. Together, they make it easier to notice, support, plan, and remember life with your person.

01 Notice each other

Say “I’m here” without starting a whole conversation.

Send a little love or share how much energy you have left today. Your partner sees the signal in a quiet update feed, so care can happen without guesswork.

  • ♥
    One-tap loveAdd a short message when words matter.
  • ↗
    Capacity check-insMake “How are you, really?” easy to answer.
  • ●
    A shared pulseRecent signals stay visible—not buried in chat.

02 Keep life moving

A shared brain for the things you both need.

Capture a weekend idea before it disappears. Keep the grocery run in one place. Notes and checklists turn all those “don’t let me forget” messages into something useful.

List Grocery run 2 of 4 done

Go on, tick one off.

03 Look forward together

Turn “we should go” into a plan you share.

Create a home for a trip, date, or big idea. Keep the dates, notes, budget, and media together—from the first spark to the memories you bring home.

“The plan doesn’t live in one person’s head anymore.”

04 Keep the good bits

Your memories, collected in context.

Photos and videos belong with the life you shared—not scattered across camera rolls and message threads. Add media to a plan or browse everything in your shared space.

One private library Photos, videos, and the story around them.

Designed with restraint

No endless feed. No strangers. No performance.

Couple Rodeo isn’t social media and it isn’t another inbox. It’s a focused space for the parts of a relationship that deserve more intention—and less noise.
