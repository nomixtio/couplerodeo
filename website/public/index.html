Everything a couple needs. Nothing more.

Small, intentional tools that help you stay close — without another messaging app demanding your attention.

Send love

One tap sends a heart straight to your partner's phone — with an optional short note. Small signal, big feeling.

Capacity check-ins

Share how much energy you have today, from 0 to 100%. Know when your partner needs space — or extra care — without asking.

Questions

Ask each other thoughtful questions — multiple choice or 1–5 scales. Learn something new about the person you know best.

Life updates

Share short updates about your day — or pick a quick premade one. Your partner can react with the perfect GIF.

Shared calendar

Plan dates, appointments, and anniversaries together — with reminders that nudge you both at the right moment.

Notes & todos

Keep simple notes and shared todo lists — groceries, trip plans, that movie you keep forgetting to watch.

Location sharing

Optionally share where you are on a simple map. Useful for "on my way home" — off by default, always your choice.

Push notifications

A real app experience: install it on your home screen and get gentle notifications when your partner reaches out.

Progressive Web App

Install it on your home screen.

Couple Rodeo is a PWA — no App Store, no download page. After you deploy, open your URL in the browser and add it to your home screen. It opens full-screen, feels like a native app, and push notifications work when it's installed (including on iPhone, with iOS 16.4+).

  • iPhone Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
  • Android / Chrome Open your URL → browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen

Calendar reminders, love signals, and check-ins — right next to your other apps.

Why is there no chat?

You already have five messaging apps. Couple Rodeo isn't another inbox — it's a quiet, structured place for the things that get lost in the scroll: how you're really doing, what you're curious about, and the small moments worth remembering. Less typing, more meaning.

Your relationship. Your data. Your server.

Couple Rodeo is MIT-licensed and built to be deployed by anyone. There's no company in the middle — your copy runs on your own Cloudflare account, and everything you share stays in your own database.

Private by architecture

Your check-ins, answers, and love notes live in a D1 database that only you control. No third party can read, sell, or lose your data.

Open source, MIT licensed

Read every line of code. Fork it, tweak the questions, change the colors, make it yours — it's your app now.

Effectively free to run

Built on Cloudflare Workers and D1. For two people, it fits comfortably in the free tier — no monthly bill for staying in love.

Fast everywhere

Deployed to Cloudflare's edge network, your app is quick whether you're at home together or continents apart.

Deploy in minutes 
# Grab the code
git clone https://github.com/nomixtio/couplerodeo
cd couplerodeo && npm install

# Create your own database
npx wrangler d1 create couplerodeo-db

# Ship it to your Cloudflare account
npm run deploy

Full setup instructions — including push notification keys — are in the README.
